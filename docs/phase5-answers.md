A1a. The tenth comes with two conditions from the book: it is cancelled when
a dissonant transit of Saturn, Uranus, Neptune or Pluto hits the natal MC,
tenth house, Ascendant, Sun or Moon in that year (TR 14, p. 162), and SRG 6
warns to avoid it unless experienced, preferring Jupiter, then Venus or the
Sun, on the SR Midheaven. The finder keeps the +30 but prints both cautions.

**A2.** The SR MC's natal house is not scored; the book never uses it.

**A3.** Intercepted signs: not in this book. Kept at +5, confirm (Treatise).

## B. The year's transits

**B4.** Transits modulate, they do not add points: SRG 5 (the SR is more
influential than transits) and TR 22 (menacing transits plus a neutral SR is
not a dramatic year; neutral transits plus a difficult SR is). Three rules
come straight from the book:
- TR 14: a dissonant Saturn, Uranus, Neptune or Pluto transit to natal MC,
  Ascendant, Sun or Moon (conjunction, square, opposition, and TR 12 adds
  semisquare and sesquisquare) cancels the natal-tenth bonus.
- TR 16: the same transits together with Ascendant, stellium, Sun or Mars in
  SR 1, 6 or 12 are "the most dramatic moments": already a hard filter, the
  explanation names the transit.
- SRG 11 and p. 175: when Saturn transits the natal 2nd or 8th that year,
  Jupiter in SR 2nd or 8th means outflow, so it is penalised (−15) instead of
  neutral.
Transit orbs for this test: TR 23 gives Sun, Moon, Venus, Mars 5°; Jupiter,
Saturn 3°; Uranus, Neptune 2°; Pluto 1°. These replace the Phase 3 defaults
(3 / 2 / 1.5) and are now in the file.

**B5.** Jupiter transits earn nothing (TR 13, TR 25).

## D. The base solar return

**D7.** No extra points. The book's goals are protection first, improvement
second (SRG 21), and SRG 19 says a trip that fixes a small thing while leaving
a big one is a waste. So each result reports what it fixes and worsens versus
the base, and the finder marks a result "marginal" when the base has no hard
problem and the gain is under 20 points.

## E. Placement rules

**E8.** The book separates hard from soft. Hard (TR 4, TR 5, SRG 2): the SR
Ascendant in natal 1/6/12, a stellium in SR 1/6/12 (including one spanning
12 and 1, SRG 3, and the 8th, SRG 9), the Sun in SR 1/6/12, Mars in SR 1/6/12.
Soft, "wise to avoid" (SRG 15): Saturn −25 (p. 177–178: never beyond sadness,
worse near the Ascendant), Uranus −20 (p. 181–182), Neptune −20 (p. 183–185),
Pluto −15 (p. 185: nothing serious unless the SR Ascendant is also in natal 1).
The Moon is minor everywhere (p. 165): −5 in 1/6/12. So the old −60/−45/−40
block is replaced by these.

**E9.** Stelliums in 1/6/12 (and 12+1, and 8): hard, not −60 (TR 4, SRG 3, 9).

**E10.** Mars and Saturn in 7 and 8 from pages 173 and 179: Mars 7 −30
(quarrels, separation, assault), Mars 8 −25 (money drain), Saturn 7 −15
(temporary troubles), Saturn 8 −25 (finances worsen). Outer planets there −10.
The full house tables for Mars, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, Pluto, Jupiter and
Venus are in the file with the page for each.

**E11.** Jupiter conjunct the MC is "the best possible influence in a Solar
Return" (p. 177) and Jupiter in the 12th "perhaps Jupiter's best position", a
guardian angel (p. 177). Bonus: Jupiter MC +30, ASC +20, DSC +15, IC +15;
Venus MC +20, ASC +15, DSC +15, IC +10; Sun MC +15 (SRG 6). Fade: full inside
5°, down to half at 12° (SRG 24 reasons with 5° orbs; your 12° is the
dominance limit). Confirm.

**E12.** Sun: 1/6/12 hard (TR 4). 2, 7, 8: "bistable", inverts the current
situation (TR 8), −10 confirm. 10 +25 (p. 162), 11 −10 (TR 10), 9 +10, 5 +10
(love goal +20), 3 and 4 0.

**E13.** "Saturn always wins over Jupiter, Venus, the Sun" (SRG 24): Saturn
within the angular orb of the MC, even from the 9th, cancels any benefic
bonus at the MC. Kept, and extended to the other three angles (confirm).

**E14.** Mars and Saturn in the same house (except 3 and 9): your rule, not in
this book. Kept as a hard filter; confirm against the Treatise.

**E15.** Dual houses: SRG 23 says a planet under 2.5° from a difficult cusp is
inside the difficult house even with a verified birth time. So the worse of
the two houses counts for penalties and hard filters; bonuses use the primary
house only.

**E16.** Added: Jupiter 12th +25 (p. 177), Venus 12th +10 (p. 171, "acts like
Jupiter at a lower level"), Venus 6th +10 (seldom negative, p. 170), Uranus
1st −10 (anxiety, p. 181), and SRG 7 and 8 as the robustness rules below.
